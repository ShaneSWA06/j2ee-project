# 🎨 Design Update: Khaki & Black Theme

## Changes Applied

### 1. **New Theme: High Contrast Khaki**

The application now features a bold **Khaki background** with high-contrast **Black typography and accents**.

**Color Palette:**

- **Background:** Khaki (#F0E68C)
- **Text:** Black (#000000)
- **Primary Buttons:** Black Background, Khaki Text
- **Secondary Buttons:** Transparent Background, Black Border, Black Text
- **Cards:** Subtle borders / light styling

### 2. **Typography**

- **Headlines:** Poppins (Black)
- **Body:** Open Sans (Black/Dark Gray)

### 3. **Design Details**

- **Borders:** Stark Black (#000000)
- **Shadows:** Hard Black shadows for a sharp, modern feel
- **Navbar:** Transparent/Khaki tint with Black text

### 🚀 **How to See Changes**

1. **Restart your server** (if needed)
2. **Hard Refresh** your browser:
   - Windows: `Ctrl + Shift + R`
   - Mac: `Cmd + Shift + R`

The application now sports a distinctive, highly accessible, and modern high-contrast look! 🖤💛

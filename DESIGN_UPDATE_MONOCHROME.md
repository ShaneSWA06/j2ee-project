# 🎨 Design Update: Minimalist Monochrome

## Overview

As per your request in `prompt.xml`, the design has been shifted to a strict **Minimalist Monochrome** system.

**Crucially, the font family (Poppins / Open Sans) has been preserved.**

## Design Tokens Applied

### Colors

- **Palette**: Pure Black (#000000) & White (#FFFFFF) only.
- **Accents**: No colored accents. Accent is achieved through black fills.
- **Backgrounds**: White or Off-white (#F5F5F5).

### Shapes & Geometry

- **Border Radius**: **0px** global. All elements have sharp 90-degree corners.
- **Shadows**: **None**. Flat design with borders for structure.
- **Glassmorphism**: Removed. Replaced with solid backgrounds.

### Components

#### Buttons

- **Primary**: Black Background, White Text. Sharp corners.
- **Secondary**: Transparent Background, Black Border.
- **Hover**: Color inversion (Black -> White, White -> Black).

#### Cards

- **Style**: Solid White background, 1px Black Border.
- **Hover**: Inverts to Black background with White text.

#### Forms

- **Inputs**: Sharp corners, 1px border.
- **Focus**: Thick (4px) bottom border black. No glow.

### Textures

- **Body**: Added subtle horizontal line pattern and noise texture for editorial feel.

## Visual Vibe

The application now feels austere, authoritative, and editorial. It mimics the layout of a high-end black-and-white magazine or gallery catalog.

### 🚀 How to See Changes

**Hard Refresh** your browser (`Ctrl + Shift + R`) to load the new `v=14` styles.
